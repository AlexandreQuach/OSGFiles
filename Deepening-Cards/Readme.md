Have you ever experienced ice-breaker moments that were too mundane, where you didn't feel like you were really connecting? 
Or conversely, have you experienced awkward moments where someone jumped too quickly to personal and deep questions?

We believe these scenarios are avoidable, and that it's not about the person but about the order in which we get to know each other, the stopping points in conversation, and practice.
What if we found a way to train ourselves by playing, to apply directly in our events?

Brise-4-Glaces (Break-4-Ice) is a simple card game designed to enable, without forcing, authentic connections within teams, organizations, and any type of collective, starting from strangers. 

Through four increasingly deeper questions, participants move from superficial conversations to more meaningful exchanges. Perfect for breaking the ice naturally and comfortably, this game helps teams get to know each other faster and build stronger relationships, making it perfect for any collaborative environment. 

The card game format guides participants through an assisted moment, but without putting one or two screens as obstacles to a relationship that's being woven.
